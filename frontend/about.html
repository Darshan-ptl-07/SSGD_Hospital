Who We Are

Healthcare with a human touch

Sanskardham Hospital is a multi-speciality hospital located in the peaceful, green campus of Sanskardham Gurukul Shree Swaminarayan Sanskardham on Gurukul Halvad Road, Dhrangadhra. We were founded with a simple belief — that every person, regardless of background, deserves quality medical care delivered with dignity and respect.

Rooted in the Swaminarayan tradition of seva (selfless service), our hospital blends modern medicine with the warmth of a family. Our doctors, nurses and staff treat every patient the way they would treat their own loved ones.

  • Multi-speciality OPD and 24/7 emergency services
  • Transparent pricing — honest advice, no unnecessary tests
  • Clean, safe and patient-friendly environment
Our Purpose

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To provide accessible, affordable and compassionate healthcare to every family in Dhrangadhra and the surrounding region — combining skilled medical expertise with the timeless value of selfless service (seva).

Our Vision

To be the most trusted community hospital in the region — a place where patients are healed with skill, treated with honesty and always welcomed with a smile.
